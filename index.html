About Us

Ikṣaṇa (इक्षण) /ik·sha·na/ – noun:
"The act of seeing, observing, or perceiving."

What began as a passionate hobby evolved into a pursuit of perfection. With every flight, every frame, and every project, we refined our craft — transforming our fascination into a vision-driven venture. Today, we're proud to bring cinematic aerial storytelling to life, one breathtaking view at a time.

We use the powerful DJI Mini 4 Pro — a compact yet professional-grade drone capable of capturing stunning 4K HDR videos and ultra-sharp photos, making every shot truly remarkable.
